Check a building plot in Denmark before you buy or build

LandSurf combines Danish national evidence for mapped surface geology, nearby boreholes and groundwater observations, adopted local plans and cadastral context into one preliminary land-screening report.

Check a plot in Denmark

What the Danish report can use

GEUS surface geology

Danmarks Digitale Jordartskort provides mapped superficial-material context. It describes mapped conditions around the site, not a parcel borehole profile.

Jupiter boreholes and groundwater

Nearby registered boreholes and water-level observations add context without being converted into parcel groundwater depth, bearing capacity or other design values.

Planning evidence

Plandata.dk is used for adopted local-plan overlap where the national service returns a valid location-specific result. The original plan remains authoritative.

Cadastral context

Danish parcel screening uses the official Matriklen/Datafordeleren service when the configured connection is available. A successful registry lookup is evidence of parcel identity and register geometry, not a new legal boundary survey.

Important limits

Screening, not design.

Automatic Danish land valuation is not shown until LandSurf has a sufficiently defensible land-only valuation source. Flood, radon and raw-material/mining matters still require separate official verification. Buildings and other improvements are excluded from land-value scope.
