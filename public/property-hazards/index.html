Property hazards: what should you check?

Find out what to check around a property before buying or building: flooding, landslides, subsidence, contamination, groundwater, geology and planning.

Ground conditions

Geology, soil, groundwater and terrain can influence foundations, excavation, drainage and settlement.

Natural hazards

Look for evidence of flooding, landslides, slope instability, erosion, subsidence, karst, seismic or volcanic processes where relevant.

Environmental and historical hazards

Historical land use and environmental records may reveal contamination or other conditions that deserve further checking.

Planning and development

Planning designations and local development controls are separate from ground hazards, but can be equally important when considering a site.

What GroundSurf can and cannot tell you

GroundSurf is a preliminary evidence browser. A positive finding needs appropriate investigation; a missing public record does not prove that a condition is absent.
