Land due diligence

Good land decisions begin with evidence

The useful question is not simply “is this a good plot?” It is “what do we actually know, from which source, at what scale, and what still needs verification?”

Start a preliminary check

Core due-diligence questions

Is the parcel correctly identified?

Check cadastral identity, boundary quality, title and rights with the competent authority.

What is beneath the site?

Use mapping, boreholes, wells and hydrogeology at their proper evidence scale.

What could constrain development?

Planning, hazards, access, utilities and environmental constraints can affect feasibility and cost.

What is the land itself worth?

Use defensible land evidence. SurveyLand excludes buildings and improvements from land value.

Fail closed when evidence is missing.

If an official source is unavailable, incomplete or not integrated, SurveyLand says so rather than filling the gap with a generic number.
