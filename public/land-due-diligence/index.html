Land due diligence, starting with public evidence

Use GroundSurf to explore public evidence for land due diligence: cadastral records, geology, ground conditions, water, hazards, planning, environment and local context.

Cadastral context

Find the parcel where official cadastral information is available and keep coverage limitations visible.

Ground and water

Explore geology, soil, terrain, groundwater and flood evidence relevant to the location.

Property and ground hazards

Look for evidence of flooding, slope instability, landslides, subsidence, erosion, karst, contamination and other locally relevant conditions.

Development context

Planning, environmental designations, access and nearby infrastructure can add important context.

From evidence to investigation

GroundSurf does not turn incomplete public data into a false certainty. It helps identify what is known and what should be checked next.
