Understand the geology beneath a piece of land

Explore geology, soil, groundwater and terrain information for land in Europe. Understand what public geological evidence may mean before development.

Geology

Identify the mapped geological context and, where available, lithology, geological units and local geological evidence.

Soil and ground

Soil and terrain information can help identify possible issues such as compressible ground, clay behaviour, peat, shallow rock or variable deposits.

Water

Groundwater and surface-water evidence can matter for foundations, excavation, drainage and flood exposure.

Terrain

Slope, elevation, aspect and geomorphology can reveal conditions that deserve closer investigation, particularly on hillsides and in mountainous regions.

Evidence is not a site investigation

Regional maps describe conditions at a useful scale but do not replace site-specific investigation.
