SurveyLand brings available geology, ground conditions, geohazards, planning and cadastral context, access and land-only value into one preliminary building-plot report.

Check a building plot

What a useful plot check separates

Ground conditions

Mapped geology and nearby observations without pretending they are a site investigation.

Geohazards

Available flood, landslide, radon, mining and other screening evidence with limitations.

Planning and cadastre

Official evidence where supported; otherwise a clear requirement for verification.

Land value

Land-only evidence where defensible. Buildings and improvements are excluded.

SurveyLand is a screening tool.

It does not replace legal due diligence, a cadastral survey, planning advice or a geotechnical investigation. Countries with only narrow or generic coverage are intentionally not given a country guide yet.
