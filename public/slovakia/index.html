What the Slovak report can cover

Mapped geology

National geological evidence is presented with its scale, source and limitations rather than converted into an invented parcel soil profile.

Borehole context

Nearby borehole records help identify what has been observed in the surrounding area, but do not prove the layer sequence beneath the whole plot.

Hydrogeology

Available national hydrogeological evidence is used for screening. Groundwater depth and engineering parameters still require site-specific verification.

Land-only value

Where the calibrated valuation path has sufficient evidence, SurveyLand shows an indicative land range. Buildings, structures and other improvements are excluded.

Decision support, not a permit or survey

Use the report to decide what deserves a closer look.

Cadastral boundaries, binding planning rules, flood constraints and geotechnical design should be checked in the relevant official records or by the appropriate professional before purchase or construction.
