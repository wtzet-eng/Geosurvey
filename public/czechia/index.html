Check a plot in Czechia

What the Czech report separates

Cadastre and parcel identity

RÚIAN/ČÚZK evidence can support parcel and building-registry context. Registry buildings are evidence only and are excluded from any land-value scope.

Geology and boreholes

Czech Geological Survey evidence provides mapped geology and nearby subsurface observations. Nearby records do not establish the parcel's full stratigraphy.

Hydrogeology

National hydrogeological evidence is used as screening context without inventing a parcel groundwater level or foundation design parameter.

Radon and mining

Where national evidence is available, LandSurf surfaces radon and mining-related screening records together with their source and limitations.

What still needs verification

Planning and engineering remain authoritative-document tasks.

Local planning, legal boundary questions and geotechnical design must be confirmed from the responsible authority or by the appropriate professional. Automatic Czech land valuation remains off rather than using a generic fallback.
